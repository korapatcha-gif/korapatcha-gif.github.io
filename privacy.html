Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

news-uploader ("the App") is operated by 4 Jakkrawan Co., Ltd., Nakhon Sawan, Thailand. The App is a private tool used by its owner to upload and manage videos on YouTube channels owned by the operator.

Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access

With the account owner's authorization (OAuth), the App accesses the owner's YouTube channel in order to upload videos, set video metadata (titles, descriptions, thumbnails), and read basic channel and video information. The App also reads public YouTube data such as search results and public video details.

How we use and store information

  • Access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer or server and are used solely to perform actions requested by the owner.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties.
  • We do not use the data for advertising.
  • Public YouTube data retrieved via the API is used only for content research and is not stored longer than necessary (no longer than 30 days).

Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access at any time from your Google Account security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. On request, we will delete any stored data associated with your account within 7 days.
